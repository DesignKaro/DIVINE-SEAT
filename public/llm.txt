## The Philosophy: Ancient Wisdom × Modern Support

For centuries, how we sit has been central to contemplative practice:
- **Asana (Steadiness & Ease)**: Inspired by Yoga Sutra 2.46 (*"Sthira sukham asanam"*), meditation requires a foundation providing unshakable stability alongside effortless ease.
- **Subtle Anatomy & Prana**: An aligned, upright spine supports open breath channels (*Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna nadis*) without chest constriction.
- **Support Allows Stillness**: Physical ease releases cognitive tension so awareness can settle inward without bodily distraction.

## Ergonomic Architecture & Engineering

Traditional meditation cushions compress the pelvis and cause lower-back slump. The Lotus Seat solves this through targeted anatomical engineering:
1. **12° Forward Pelvic Incline**: Restores natural lumbar lordosis, stacking vertebrae vertically with zero muscular exertion.
2. **Ischial Tuberosity Troughs**: Bilateral depressions distribute weight evenly across sit bones, removing focal pressure spikes.
3. **Perineal & Coccyx Relief Channel**: Recessed groove relieves pressure on delicate soft tissues, pelvic floor nerves, and the tailbone.
4. **Sub-Ankle Clearance**: Gentle elevation prevents ankle and foot compression on hard floors, maintaining healthy circulation.

## Sustainable, Non-Toxic Materials

- **Foundation**: Handcrafted high-density 100% renewable Portuguese cork. Naturally antimicrobial, hypoallergenic, sound-dampening, and slip-resistant.
- **Cushion Core**: 100% natural botanical latex. Dual-density responsive resilience that never compacts or sags.
- **Upholstery**: Removable, machine-washable plush velvet cover with gold embroidered lotus motif.
- **Zero Toxins**: Free from polyurethane foams, petrochemical off-gassing, flame retardants, or harmful adhesives.

## Technical Specifications

- **Dimensions**: 450 mm (width) × 330 mm (depth) × 165 mm (peak rear elevation)
- **Weight**: Approx. 2.8 kg (stable floor anchor yet portable)
- **Weight Capacity**: Tested to support practitioners up to 160 kg (350 lbs)
- **Supported Postures**: Burmese cross-legged, Siddhasana, Ardha Padmasana (half-lotus), Padmasana (full lotus), kneeling seiza, and open gentle cross-legged posture.
