  3. **Pressure Buildup**: Sitting for extended durations concentrates pressure beneath the tailbone and ischial tuberosities (sitting bones).
  4. **Constant Readjustment**: When the foundation is unstable, attention repeatedly fractures to adjust posture, breaking meditative depth.

### Section 13: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. **Is The Lotus Seat suitable for beginners who cannot sit cross-legged?**
   *Yes. The elevation and slope significantly reduce hip tightness, allowing beginners with tight hips to sit comfortably in Sukhasana (easy cross-legged) or half-lotus.*
2. **How does this prevent leg numbness?**
   *Leg numbness is caused by nerve and vascular compression. By elevating the pelvis above the knees, blood flow and femoral nerve paths remain uncompressed.*
3. **Why do you use natural latex instead of memory foam?**
   *Memory foam compresses slowly, creates heat traps, and sinks completely under body weight. Natural latex is instantly responsive, buoyant, cooling, and does not lose its shape.*
4. **How do I clean and wash the seat?**
   *The outer linen cover unzips effortlessly and can be machine-washed on a gentle cold cycle and air-dried.*
